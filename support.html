📱 About InstaScan

InstaScan helps you scan, recognize, extract, and manage text from images using advanced OCR technology. Whether you're digitizing documents, receipts, notes, or screenshots, InstaScan makes text capture simple and fast.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Camera is not working

Make sure Camera permission is enabled: Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera → InstaScan.


OCR accuracy is low

Use a clear image with good lighting and avoid blurry or tilted photos.


App is running slowly

Close background applications and ensure you are running the latest version of InstaScan.


App crashes unexpectedly

Restart your device and update InstaScan from the App Store.
